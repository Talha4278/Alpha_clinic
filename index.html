Prosthodontics & Surgery

Dental Implants & Bone Management

Dental implants are the gold standard for replacing missing teeth. Led by Consultant Prosthodontist Dr. Umar Farooq (BDS, FCPS), our clinic offers biocompatible titanium implants, immediate load implants, and advanced bone grafting for long-term chewing comfort and natural aesthetics.

  • Single tooth, multiple teeth, or full arch restoration
  • Bone grafting & sinus lift management for low bone volume
  • Permanent solution lasting 20+ years with proper oral hygiene
Specialist: Dr. Umar Farooq (FCPS) Inquire Implants
Endodontics

Painless Root Canal Treatment (RCT)

When tooth decay or infection reaches the nerve, a root canal saves the natural tooth from extraction. Using modern rotary files, digital apex locators, and potent local anesthesia, Dr. Farhan Raza delivers fast, gentle, and virtually pain-free root canal therapy.

  • Single-visit treatment option for suitable clinical cases
  • Complete relief from severe toothache and sensitivity
  • Finished with high-strength Zirconium or PFM crowns
Specialist: Dr. M. Farhan Raza (RDS) Book RCT Visit
Cosmetic Dentistry

Smile Design, Veneers & Zirconium Crowns

Transform chipped, discolored, misaligned, or worn-down teeth into a radiant, harmonious smile. We design bespoke porcelain veneers, composite veneers, and metal-free Zirconium crowns crafted to suit your unique facial symmetry and personal aesthetic preferences.

  • Digital smile evaluation and shade matching
  • Ultra-thin porcelain veneers with minimal enamel preparation
  • Translucent Zirconium crowns for unmatched natural brilliance
Specialist: Dr. Umar Farooq & Dr. Farhan Raza Consult Smile Design
Preventative & Esthetics

Professional Teeth Whitening & Ultrasonic Scaling

Remove stubborn tea, coffee, and tobacco stains while protecting tooth enamel. Our in-office bleaching safely lifts discoloration by multiple shades in a single 45-minute session, paired with gentle ultrasonic calculus removal for fresh breath and pink gums.

  • Immediate visible brightening in a single clinical visit
  • Ultrasonic scaling removes plaque without harming enamel
  • Desensitizing agents applied for comfortable post-care
Specialist: Dr. M. Farhan Raza (RDS) Book Whitening
Orthodontics

Orthodontic Braces & Clear Invisible Aligners

Correct crowded teeth, open bites, gaps, and malocclusion for both teens and adults. We provide traditional ceramic/metal brackets as well as removable invisible clear aligners that straighten your teeth discreetly without dietary restrictions.

  • Discreet, transparent clear aligners custom-modeled for your bite
  • Removable during meals, brushing, and special events
  • Comprehensive bite alignment for long-term TMJ health
Specialist: Alpha Dental Orthodontic Team Aligner Assessment
Oral Surgery

Wisdom Tooth Surgery & Safe Extractions

Impacted or infected third molars can cause severe pain, swelling, and crowding of adjacent teeth. Our surgical team performs gentle, minimally traumatic wisdom tooth extractions with precision suturing and comprehensive post-operative recovery guidance.

  • Safe removal of vertically, horizontally, or angularly impacted teeth
  • Minimal swelling technique with modern local anesthesia
  • Emergency walk-in extractions for acute tooth pain
Specialist: Dr. Umar Farooq & Dr. Farhan Raza Surgical Consultation

Do you offer pain-free dental treatments?

Yes. We use advanced local anesthesia, fine-gauge needles, and gentle rotary instruments to ensure procedures like root canal treatment, extractions, and dental implant placements are comfortable and virtually painless.

How much does a dental implant cost in Islamabad?

The cost of a dental implant varies depending on individual bone structure, the implant brand chosen, and whether additional bone grafting is needed. We offer competitive, transparent pricing with no hidden charges. Contact us on WhatsApp for an assessment and detailed cost breakdown.

How long does professional teeth whitening last?

In-office teeth whitening results typically last from 1 to 3 years depending on dietary habits (such as consumption of tea, coffee, or smoking) and personal oral hygiene maintenance.

What is the difference between traditional braces and clear aligners?

Traditional braces use fixed brackets and wires for comprehensive alignment. Clear aligners are custom-made, transparent plastic trays that are removable when eating or brushing, offering a nearly invisible orthodontic alternative.
